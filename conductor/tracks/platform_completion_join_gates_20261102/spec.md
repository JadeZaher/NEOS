# Objective and scope

The active goal text is truncated ("this this being complete and tested as a"). The surrounding audit and the owner's clarification establish the working scope: finish the listed user-facing stubs and usability gaps, test actual ordinary-member/steward/NodeAdmin journeys, enforce configured onboarding quizzes for ecosystem and relevant subgroup participation, and provide a clear administration destination for managing the platform. Preserve this complete scope until verified; do not close the goal on a smaller passing subset.

The owner explicitly selected **require quizzes only where one is configured**. A scope with no configured onboarding quiz remains joinable. A configured graded quiz needs an authentic passing result for the actual entrant. A nongraded onboarding questionnaire needs authentic server-verified completion with valid answers; personal preferences and availability are not right-or-wrong tests. An unpublished/unavailable configured requirement must not silently open the gate.

The owner subsequently clarified that **platform admins can bypass quizzes and assign ecosystem roles where configured**. An explicit active NodeAdmin assignment exempts that entrant from quiz requirements without inventing a passing attempt. The exemption does not follow from builder/co-creator profiles or local ecosystem roles. Support role changes use the actual configured role choices and retain public audit receipts. Ordinary entrants still satisfy the configured gate unless an admin deliberately uses a reasoned, audited support override.

## Functional acceptance

1. Ecosystem signup/join/rejoin/activation, domain/circle participation, team membership and agent join actions share server-enforced quiz prerequisites, with the explicit NodeAdmin exemption above. Applicable ancestor and target quizzes are explained with names, status and links. Existing active memberships are not destructively demoted. Agreement consent and applicable quiz requirements both hold before activation.
2. Entrants can read/take the published required quiz before gaining participation. Answer keys are not disclosed before submission, grading/completion verification is server-owned, and client-supplied scores/identities/old unrelated attempts cannot bypass the gate. Nongraded completion validates the configured required questions and answer shapes/choices, counting false as a valid boolean. Legacy questionnaires without required flags need valid answers to every answerable question. Empty, unknown or malformed answers cannot manufacture completion, and completion does not manufacture a passing grade. Tests cover the existing readiness questionnaire shape, no quiz, published pass/fail, replacement/current content, unpublished requirements, both agreement/quiz completion orders, scope boundaries, rejoin, steward actions and agent/API bypass attempts.
3. Assigned NodeAdmins have an obvious persistent administration entry and task navigation for users, ecosystems, memberships/subgroups, onboarding/quizzes and audit. It remains reachable with no membership or pending personal onboarding. Technical support changes remain reasoned, reviewed and publicly audited; ordinary governance participation is not fabricated.
4. Orientation OmniBot is a real, bounded, tool-free assistant using the configured paid non-Anthropic provider. Private owner history is separated from governance/support sessions. Availability/errors are honest before typing; canned or failed replies do not count as learning completion.
5. Participant discovery and ecosystem fit use the binding matching guides: consent, governed versioned profiles, adequate answer coverage, transparent reasons and immutable/stale assessment evidence. No fabricated similarity claims or disclosure of private profiles. Missing data has a useful completion/configuration path.
6. Ordinary member agent tools complete authorized messaging, collaboration proposal/discovery, governance audit edit/publish/abandon, exit follow-through/export, and conflict/repair lifecycle operations. Reuse the REST services/policies; bind actual actor/scope, preserve ambiguity handling, exact frozen confirmations and one-use execution. NodeAdmin correction tools remain separate.
7. Stewards can author and preview orientation journeys through structured step/exit-package forms with inline validation. NodeAdmins can choose related records by recognizable names and edit known structured fields without mandatory raw JSON. An advanced representation must not be the sole normal path.
8. Public audit timestamps serialize timezone-aware UTC and display correctly across zones/DST, preserving original records and private snapshot round trips.
9. Verified-unused legacy stubs are removed or isolated without deleting working features. Plain-language, video fallback/playback and accessibility findings receive meaningful fixes/verification rather than being hidden by revised metrics.
