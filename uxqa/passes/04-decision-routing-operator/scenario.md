# Situation

A member moves between governance explanations, practical platform help and a concrete governance process. They expect the operator to choose appropriate guidance, act only on their instructions, and keep the conversation available while they follow links or close the drawer.

## Focus

- Guided skill -> direct platform/framework answer -> a new process, with no stale skill.
- Correct current advice policy, scoped reads, useful unresolved-record guidance, simple language, full exit rights and steps, ecosystem clarification, and drafts that leave unspecified commitments unspecified.
- Drawer continuity, route labels, safe visible refusals, neutral confirmation wording, limit recovery, keyboard access and 390px geometry.
- All seven gold tasks. Orientation must reach the actual journey, including join-first behavior where applicable. Repeat answers count as delivered when the UI offers Open the conversation after a successful send.

## Evidence and constraints

Target is the isolated localhost study stack and neos_uxqa. The coordinator proved both health and database sentinel before starting. Re-prove before live journeys. Persona journeys are UI-only, have finite attempt/time budgets, call finish, and retain traces. API fixture preparation and model capability probes are recorded separately and never count as UX success.

Retain the existing 16 seeded identities so legacy regressions remain reproducible; the current research cast and constrained journeys are identified in personas.json. No external messages are sent outside the local QA accounts. At most two live assistant messages per UI lane; the separately budgeted operator eval is a capability probe.

Open research questions from pass03 remain in scope: whether members understand activation/consent, exchange terminology, visibility and exit outcomes. A new route badge is not evidence that the response is correct.
