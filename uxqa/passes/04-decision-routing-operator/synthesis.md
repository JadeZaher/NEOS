The new routing and conversation UI support the intended operator experience, but this pass does not meet the study's exit criteria. Live journeys visibly switched between a guided ACT skill, platform help and framework help, with history and unsent drafts preserved across navigation. The finalized capability evaluation passed 18 of 26 tasks (69%), compared with 14 of 25 (56%) in pass 03. The task set changed, so this is a directional comparison rather than a controlled model improvement claim.

## Evidence and interpretation

- **Process correctness:** `operator-ui-lane.md` and the R4 transcript establish unsupported advice-deadline/automatic-transition claims. Correct routing alone does not ensure correct facts. Canonical skill text, tool lifecycle facts and runtime precedence need to agree. Recheck both an explicitly guided explanation and a direct historical question in pass 05.
- **Navigation and discoverability:** transcript/draft continuity, readable per-message routing and Messaging availability passed the observed desktop/mobile journeys. The profile answer invented generic avatar/Edit controls, while the visible route is Me → Profile → Display name → Save profile. Supply bounded, verified platform context and retest the actual destination.
- **Action reliability:** C4 failed to withdraw after confirmation, and C5 confused groups with ecosystems. Freeze reviewed arguments server-side, reauthorize at execution and preserve bounded record references; inspect actual persisted outcomes after confirmation. The three raw state-violation counters are missed intended writes in C3/C4/C5, not observed unauthorized additional writes.
- **Inclusive language:** the constrained lane's consent/exit answers were complete, while P1/P2 exceeded the grade-8 heuristic. Keep shorter sentences and explain one next action. The heuristic supports, but does not replace, testing with readers.
- **Journey measurement:** four gold tasks completed on their first run; advise, orientation and response completed only after corrected driver runs. The original failures remain included: those three tasks score 50%, below 80%. Extra attempts exceeded the strict journey budget. Aggregate friction rose to 6 from pass 03's 2.54, largely from these retained failures; do not erase them or present seven uninterrupted successes.
- **Harness validity:** A6 collided with an archived same-name R5 fixture; C3 omitted a required change/rationale; P3 rejected the correct “30-day” wording. Correct these measurement defects without rewriting the historical result. New fixture namespaces and authoritative created-proposal readback make later evidence more useful.
- **Accessibility and trust:** the natural keyboard-only orientation lane progressed; mobile drawer bounds and close-focus return passed. Other gold keyboard actions used locator-assisted focus. No axe scan ran, and the failed video had a working fallback. These observations do not establish complete keyboard or WCAG compliance.

## Research still needed

Conduct five human sessions per segment with new members who use simple language, keyboard-only users, and cross-organization support operators. Observe whether people understand routed-help labels, differentiate a pending confirmation from a completed action, choose the correct organization, and find their saved conversation. Record first attempts and abandonment without driver assistance. Investigate the failed welcome-video resource under a normal network before changing its source; this restricted test environment cannot establish its cause.

Native UI/probe/review lanes replaced the unavailable Workflow tool. This document synthesizes their evidence; it is not a claim that a seven-agent research panel ran.
