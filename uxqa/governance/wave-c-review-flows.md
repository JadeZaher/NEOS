# Wave C review: do the flows work end to end, and do client and server agree?

## Verdict

REQUEST_CHANGES. Most of the wave holds up. Onboarding placement, the consent gate, steward moves, posts, respond, fulfil and reopen, hide and restore, delete, collaboration approvals, review outcomes, rollback and the health snapshot all match between page and route, and the suites are green: backend wave tests 393 passed and 1 skipped, `npm run check` exit 0, `npm test` 92/92.

Three flows still break for real users, each confirmed by a probe or by reading the code:

1. A poster who belongs to two ecosystems is told someone answered their post, but cannot open the answer while another ecosystem is selected. The item never clears.
2. An admin or owner who has not consented to an agreement in force gets a "Record your consent to this version" button. It opens an "Archive this agreement?" dialog and fails with a raw 400.
3. The collaboration review item tells stewards to "continue it", but nothing can record that. It stays as an overdue item at the top of every steward's queue until the collaboration is ended.

Several MEDIUM gaps contradict owner defaults:

- A returning leaver goes straight to active without consenting again.
- A sunset can be archived the minute it starts.
- One abandoned audit draft silences the audit-due reminder for good.
- The domain-steward rule can be bypassed through a free-text field.

## Flows walked and found sound

- Sign-up, then placed, then the consent gate. `register` sends `ecosystem_id` (`AuthContext.tsx` register into `registerAccount`). `signup_ecosystem` returns 404 for a private ecosystem (`api/auth.py:105-108`). `place_member` writes one transition (`member_lifecycle.py:144-157`). `/auth/me` reports `membership_status` per ecosystem, and `activeEcosystemIds` reads it (`lib/consent-gate.ts:32-38`).
- Gate, then orientation, then consent, then active. `/orientation/...` is readable while gated (`consent-gate.ts:26`). Pending rows may save progress. Consent activates through `record_transition` (`api/agreements.py:834-836`). `useAttestAgreementConsent` refreshes the session (`hooks/use-governance.ts:47-59`).
- Joining items reach a pending-only newcomer through REST (probe P3: `consent_to_join`, linked to `/agreements/<id>`, which is readable while gated). The stuck case gets `finish_joining`, then `/welcome/consent`, then "Check my membership", then `gate_cleared` (`ConsentGatePage.tsx:106-125`).
- First-month items: `start_orientation` links to `/orientation/<ecosystem id>` (the route resolves an id, `api/orientation.py:89`). `take_entry_quiz` links to `/quiz/take/<id>`. Both clear when done (`test_onboarding_flows.py:409-426`).
- Joining a second ecosystem: `useJoinEcosystem` reads 409 `ALREADY_MEMBER` / `CONSENT_REQUIRED` as results (`lib/ecosystem-join.ts:53-62`) and invalidates `onboardingAffects.joinEcosystem`. EcosystemDetail shows "Waiting on your consent" for pending (`EcosystemDetail.tsx`).
- A steward fixes a stuck newcomer. `stuck_newcomer_items` links to `/members/<id>`. The profile returns `caller_member_moves` from the same `move_refusal` the route uses (`member_lifecycle.py:181-223`). MembershipMovesCard confirms with the server's sentence and shows the 409 `required_agreements` inline. `memberMove` invalidates nextSteps and onboarding.
- Exchange, posting through answering: post, a member of another ecosystem finds it, respond, unread count and queue item, fulfil naming a responder, reopen. This holds for single-selection users (`test_exchange_flows.py:43-93`). The UI draws moves from `allowed_next` (`lib/exchange.ts:121-127`), lists responders for the author only, and checks `NOT_A_RESPONDER` on the server.
- Exchange, moderation and deletion. Hide needs a reason (client `useRequiredFields` plus server `REASON_REQUIRED`). Restore is steward-only. Delete is author-only and unanswered-only (`caller_can_delete` matches `can_delete_publication`). `affects.exchangePosts` refreshes lists, details, profiles, the queue and conversations.
- Collaboration up to activation and ending. A steward proposer auto-approves the source side only cross-ecosystem. `caller_can_approve_for` comes from `_may_approve`, the same predicate as the route and the queue item. Activation defaults `review_date` to +365. End needs a reason, and `end_outcomes` comes from the server (`CollaborationDetail.tsx:40-76`).
- Agreement review. `start_review` appears 14 days ahead for admins and owners. Keep in force needs findings (8+ characters, client and server). `next_review_date` must be later than today (AoE). It writes a `ReviewRecord` and the new date, and the History tab shows findings, conductor, version and timing (`api/agreements.py:417-439`; `AgreementHistory.tsx`).
- Rollback is limited to draft and advice (`api/agreements.py:1126-1129`), and the button follows `caller_can_rollback`.
- Health from snapshot to members. The snapshot answers 404 off-ecosystem. `from > to` is 400, so there is no zero-day window. Each indicator matches `indicator-definitions.yaml` bands and formulas (GHI-01 unique/total, GHI-02 non-leadership/leadership with `lead_ok == 0` guarded, GHI-04 prior == 0 guarded, GHI-07 timely/due, GHI-08 normalised per 90 days). Every "needs attention" division is guarded, and every count filters by `ecosystem_id`, with no leakage (GHI-08 filters shared ids in Python). Publish refuses `RATINGS_MISSING` naming the indicators, freezes all eight rows with prior and trend, and is checked again against the auditor rule. The per-ecosystem summary shows published audits only. The critical rating produces `respond_to_audit`, and self-assessment is labelled (`AuditDetail.tsx` badge).
- Queue items. No uid collisions across sources: action, record and part are distinct. `scope.wanted` is respected (callers are pre-filtered and `joining_items` checks `wanted`). Every link resolves to an `App.tsx` route (`/members/:id`, `/orientation/:id`, `/quiz/take/:id`, `/welcome/consent`, `/agreements/:id`, `/discover?tab=shares-needs&post=`, which DiscoverHub opens, `/discover/collaborations/:id`, `/safeguards`, `/safeguards/audits/:id`), except the known `/messaging?conversation=` and F1.
- The date rule's core. `dates.aoe_today` and `date_passed` are correct at the boundary (due 1 Nov becomes passed at 2 Nov 12:00 UTC). `Clock` uses it. The UTC pin covers the app and the MCP engine (`db/session.py:26-41`, `mcp_server.py:107`). NotificationCenter and Dashboard pass the server's `overdue` to `dueStatus`.

## Hand-offs that are wrong or understated

- **"MessagingLayout `?conversation=` not handled": understated.** Even with the parameter handled, `GET /messaging/conversations/:id` returns 404 when the post's ecosystem is not selected, which is the default for any multi-ecosystem user (F1). The fix is server-side (messaging scope), not only the layout.
- **"domain form needs a `reason` field": understated.** DomainForm never sends `steward_id`. It edits the free-text `current_steward`, which is what DomainDetail displays (`DomainDetail.tsx:236`), and the server does not guard it (`AUTHORITY_FIELDS` covers only `steward_id` and `status`, `api/domains.py`). A domain's own steward can therefore still "hand on" their domain, as members see it, with no reason and no DecisionRecord, while the C10 rule guards a field no page edits. The form needs a member picker for `steward_id` plus the reason, and `current_steward` should be server-derived (refuse it in `PUT`). Separately, any status change from the form is impossible for everyone today: the domain steward gets 403 and other stewards get 400 with no field to fill.
- **"`act_gates` advice-deadline date lines -> `dates.aoe_today`": incomplete.** The same server-local `date.today()` decisions remain in G-Exchange (`services/exchange.py:765,846,916,959`, F9), in `api/conflicts.py:126,156` (case-detail check-in overdue), and in chat `governance_tools.py:2427` (pre-confirmation close-advice refusal).
- **Not on the list but should be:** the C7 "a proposal that cites the audit clears `respond_to_audit`" path (`/proposals/new?responds_to_audit=`, proposals schema) was dropped without a recorded hand-off (F7).
